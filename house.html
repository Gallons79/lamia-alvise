A restored Apulian lamia surrounded by olive trees.

Our Stone Lamia

Lamia Alvise is a traditional Apulian stone house, carefully restored to preserve its original charm while offering modern comfort. Thick stone walls keep the interior naturally cool, and the surrounding olive grove creates a peaceful, intimate atmosphere.

Exterior

The lamia features traditional white stone, soft Mediterranean lines, and a private outdoor area surrounded by olive trees and dry stone walls.

Interior

Inside, the lamia offers a cozy and bright atmosphere, with natural materials, soft colors, and handcrafted details that reflect Apulian tradition.

Surroundings

The lamia is immersed in a quiet rural landscape, with ancient olive trees, dry stone walls, and the warm light typical of the Apulian countryside.
