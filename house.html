The House

A traditional Apulian lamia, restored with stone, light, and simplicity.

Architecture

Lamia Alvise is a small stone house typical of the Apulian countryside, with thick walls, vaulted ceilings, and a naturally cool interior even in summer.

The restoration preserved the original character while adding modern comfort: natural materials, soft colors, and simple lines.
