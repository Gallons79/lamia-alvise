The Surroundings

Discover Apulia’s landscapes, towns, and coastline.

Explore the Area

Lamia Alvise is surrounded by the beauty of the Apulian countryside — a place where olive groves, whitewashed towns, and crystal‑clear beaches are all within easy reach.

Nearby Towns

Explore charming Apulian towns such as Ostuni, Cisternino, and Ceglie Messapica. Wander through narrow alleys, admire whitewashed houses, and enjoy local cuisine in traditional trattorias.

Beaches

Whether you prefer sandy beaches or rocky coves, fully equipped beach clubs or untouched stretches of coastline immersed in pristine nature, our coast offers something for every taste.

Experiences

Enjoy local traditions such as olive oil tastings, bicycle tours, patronal festivals, and visits to historic masserie. Apulia offers a rich blend of culture, nature, and tradition.
