Booking Conditions

Minimum notice: 3 days before arrival.

Discounted rates available for stays longer than 7 nights.

A 30% deposit is required to confirm the reservation.

Tourist Tax

The Municipality of Carovigno applies a tourist tax of €1.30 per person per night not included in the daily rate. The tax is calculated for a maximum of 7 nights, even for longer stays.

For official regulations, exemptions, and updates, please refer to the Carovigno municipality website .
